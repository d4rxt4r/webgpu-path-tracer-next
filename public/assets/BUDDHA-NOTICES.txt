Happy Buddha model
Source: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory
Stanford 3D Scanning Repository
https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/
https://graphics.stanford.edu/pub/3Dscanrep/happy/happy_recon.tar.gz

This project distributes the full reconstruction for a free research/demo
renderer. Stanford allows research use and free mirroring/redistribution with
source attribution. Commercial use, including inclusion in a product for sale,
requires permission from Stanford. The full terms are on the repository page
under "Please acknowledge..." and "Inappropriate uses of these models".
Credit rendered images to the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory.

The full happy_recon/happy_vrip.ply source (543652 vertices, 1087716 triangles)
is converted to assets/Buddha.obj by scripts/export-buddha-original.py.
Only the file format changes: original vertex coordinates, face indices and
components are preserved. There is no decimation, voxel remesh or speck removal.
Archive and OBJ hashes are recorded in assets/Buddha-source.json.
At runtime the OBJ importer centers and scales the mesh and skips zero-area
triangles, reporting their count. Optional hole repair is controlled by the UI.
Procedural marble/lava appearances are renderer materials, not scan textures.
