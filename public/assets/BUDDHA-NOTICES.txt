Happy Buddha model
Source: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory
Stanford 3D Scanning Repository
https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/
https://graphics.stanford.edu/pub/3Dscanrep/happy/happy_recon.tar.gz

This project distributes a simplified reconstruction for a free research/demo
renderer. Stanford allows research use and free mirroring/redistribution with
source attribution. Commercial use, including inclusion in a product for sale,
requires permission from Stanford. The full terms are on the repository page
under "Please acknowledge..." and "Inappropriate uses of these models".
Credit rendered images to the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory.

Prepared using scripts/prepare-buddha.py (Blender 4.4): decimation, normal
recalculation, centering, uniform scale, 3mm voxel reconstruction to resolve
degenerate scan bridges, and removal of seven disconnected scan specks.
Procedural marble/lava appearances are renderer materials, not original scan
textures. The geometry does not melt, break or change shape.
