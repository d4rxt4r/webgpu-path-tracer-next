Suzanne OBJ models

The original Suzanne primitive is exported from Blender 4.4.3 by
scripts/export-suzanne-original.py to assets/suzanne-original.obj.
It preserves Blender's native render tessellation and f32 coordinates, with
an axis conversion to Y-up. No subdivision, remesh, smoothing or union is applied.
assets/Suzanne.obj is the user-provided high poly Blender Suzanne export.
Both source OBJ files are distributed unchanged. Normalization and optional
hole repair happen separately in the application's OBJ worker.
